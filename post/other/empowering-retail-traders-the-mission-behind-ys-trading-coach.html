From Confusion to Consistent Profitability Through Price Action and Volume Mastery ​

The stock market holds immense potential. Yet for most new or uninformed retail traders, the dream of financial independence through trading often turns into a frustrating struggle. Many spend two to three years searching for the "holy grail" of trading indicators, falling prey to internet scams, or blindly following so-called mentors who sell dreams but not results.

This is the unfortunate reality — and it’s exactly what YS Trading Coach is on a mission to change.

Why Most Retail Traders Fail ​

Let’s be honest — the majority of beginners in the stock market don’t truly understand how price moves or how volume reflects the intentions of big market players. They jump from free YouTube videos to social media tips, trying to piece together a trading strategy. More often than not, this leads to:

  • Falling for fake indicators that work only a few times.
  • Losing money due to a lack of risk management.
  • Buying cheap courses filled with surface-level theory.
  • Losing trust in the education space or quitting trading altogether.

What they need is structured guidance, backed by real experience — not noise.

My Journey from Losses to Consistency ​

My trading journey began in 2015. I paid ₹18,000 to a broker who promised to teach me everything about the stock market. The course lasted just two days, with basic coverage of equity markets and chart patterns. There was no mention of stop-loss, volume dynamics, or institutional behavior. My first trade resulted in an unexpected brokerage charge of ₹300 — something my broker conveniently never disclosed. That day, I realized the system was not built in favor of retail traders.

Over the years, I lost money — not due to market conditions, but due to lack of knowledge. I explored options, futures, forex, and crypto, always searching for answers. In 2020, when the pandemic hit and I lost my job, I committed full-time to trading with my savings of just ₹1,00,000.

I read over 60+ books, studied price action, and discovered how volume reveals the actions of institutional traders. Key resources like YTC Price Action Trader by Lance Beggs, Volume Price Analysis by Anna Coulling, and Volume Profile: Insider's Guide by Trader Dale transformed my perspective.

Through back-testing, practice, and disciplined learning, I achieved consistent profitability by late 2021.
