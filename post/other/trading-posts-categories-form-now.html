Trading Posts Categories Form Now ​

July 31, 2025

This post is me explaining how upcoming post will be categorized and who will benefit for these posts so lets begin with these answers below

Table of Contents

  • Who Are These Posts For?
  • Post Categorized
  • Comments

Who Are These Posts For? ​

Well this is simple to answer, it's for everyone whether you are new to trading and searching for course or strategy in trading my role in this is to perhaps provide some guidance along the way, keeping you on the right path and minimizing the distractions and false turns.

More of a mentor or coach, than teacher. I can point you in the right direction. Success or failure though, is up to you.

An important factor is whether or not you relate to both my style of teaching and my style of trading. So read these posts, watch some of my YouTube videos link here, register to my site to get the weekly newsletter (updates).

If you like the way I work by reading the posts, free course articles and if my trading style seems compatible with yours, then perhaps there’s a chance you could take my paid course.

If your underlying beliefs about how markets operate are different from mine, then once again you may be better seeking guidance elsewhere.

So these posts will help novice traders know my style and paid users deepen their course knowledge further with current market examples.

Post Categorized ​

  1. Other – In this category we include all articles of a general nature, which do not fit within the Trader, Trading Business or Trading Process and Strategy categories.
  2. Trader - In this category our interest is in exploring all aspects of peak human performance, including:
    1. Examination of the human body and mind and the ways that they impact upon our trading results, both positively and negatively.
    2. Exploration of learning theory and the ways to maximize the development of knowledge and skill.
  3. Trading Business – In this category we explore the business side of our trading endeavor. This includes topics such as:
    1. Market and timeframe selection,
    2. trading procedures – pre, during and post-session,
    3. The journal and review processes,
    4. Money Management,
    5. Risk Management,
    6. Hardware and Software,
    7. The office environment.
  4. Trading Process and Strategy – In this category we discuss all aspects of the trading process, including:
    1. Technical analysis,
    2. Trade Strategy,
    3. Identification of trade opportunity,
    4. Trade entry,
    5. Trade management and exit.
  5. Trading Concepts – In this category we examine the foundational and advanced principles that drive price action and volume across all timeframes, shaped by institutional trading activity. This includes topics such as:
    1. Market structure and liquidity,
    2. Fair value gaps and trend shifts,
    3. Timeframe alignment,
    4. Premium/discount models,
    5. Market Maker models,
    6. Daily profiles

These posts provide practical insights into the frameworks and concepts used by professional traders, with guidance on applying them in real-time to identify high-probability trading opportunities.

That's all folks!

see you in next week of August.
