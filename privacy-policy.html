Privacy Policy ​

Last updated: July 26, 2025

Our website address is: https://ystc.in/.

At YS Trading Coach, we are committed to protecting your privacy as a customer and online visitor.

We use the information we collect to provide and improve our services. Please read this Privacy Policy carefully to understand how your data is collected, used, and protected.

Information We Collect ​

During your visits to our website or when using our services, we may collect:

  • Name and company name
  • Email address and telephone number
  • Billing and mailing address
  • Geographic location and IP address
  • Survey responses and support queries
  • Comments and social media handles

Note: Our services are not directed to persons under 18. If we discover that a child has submitted data, we will promptly delete it. Parents or guardians may contact us if they believe this has occurred.

You may review, correct, update, or delete your data by logging into your account or contacting us directly.

How We Use Your Information ​

Personally Identifiable Information (PII):

  • Communicate with you about services
  • Provide technical support
  • Send updates, offers, or newsletters (only if you opt in)
  • Personalize your website experience
  • Diagnose issues and measure customer satisfaction

Non-Personally Identifiable Information (Non-PII):

  • Administer and improve the website
  • Create reports and analytics
  • Understand customer needs and preferences
  • Market products and services in aggregate

Public content (such as testimonials or blog comments) may remain visible in cached/archived copies even if you delete it.

Storage and Security ​

  • Personal Data is stored on secure servers with restricted access.
  • Credit card details are encrypted before transmission and never stored.
  • Data is retained only as long as necessary to provide services or meet legal requirements.
  • In case of a security breach, we will notify you promptly as required by law.

Cookies ​

Our website uses cookies to:

  • Analyze traffic
  • Enable social media sharing features
  • Improve user experience

Cookies do not access your personal files. You can disable them in your browser, but this may affect site functionality.

Sharing Your Information ​

We do not sell or trade Personal Data.

We may share data only when:

  • Required by law
  • Needed to process payments or deliver services
  • To protect our rights, trademarks, or intellectual property

Any third party we share data with must agree to comply with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

Legal Disclosure ​

We may disclose information if required:

  • By law, regulation, or legal process (court order, subpoena, etc.)
  • To protect the safety and rights of YS Trading Coach, our customers, or others
  • In the event of a merger, acquisition, or transfer of assets (under confidentiality agreements)

Links to Other Websites ​

Our website may contain third-party links.

  • These are for your convenience only.
  • We are not responsible for their privacy practices.
  • Please review their policies before submitting personal data.

GDPR Compliance (EU & UK Residents) ​

If you are in the EU or UK, you have rights under GDPR.

Our lawful bases for processing include:

  • Fulfilling a contract with you (e.g., sending goods/services)
  • Valid consent (e.g., newsletters or notifications)
  • Legitimate interests (e.g., analytics, service improvement)

Your Rights (EU & UK) ​

As an EU/UK resident, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how data is used
  • Access your data
  • Request corrections or updates
  • Object to processing
  • Request restriction of processing
  • Request deletion (“right to be forgotten”)
  • Request data portability
  • Not be subject to automated decision-making

To exercise your rights, contact us.

Withdrawing Consent ​

  • You may withdraw consent at any time by unsubscribing from emails or contacting us.
  • Withdrawal may affect access to our services.
  • We may still process data if required for legal compliance or legitimate security reasons.

Compliance & Safeguards ​

  • All Personal Data is treated as confidential.
  • We use encryption, pseudonymization, and strong cyber security measures.
  • Only authorized personnel have access to Personal Data.
  • Data is collected only for specific purposes and retained no longer than necessary.

Your Acknowledgement ​

By providing us with your Personal Data, you consent to its transfer outside the EU/UK, provided that third parties are GDPR-compliant.

Contact Us ​

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your Personal Data, please contact us. We aim to respond within 48 hours.

Changes to Privacy Policy ​

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be effective immediately upon posting. Please check back periodically for updates.
