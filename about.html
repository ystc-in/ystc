About ​

YS Trading Coach was founded with the vision of helping aspiring traders and investors think and trade like professionals.
Our mission is to guide everyday people toward becoming successful traders through knowledge, strategies, and informed decision-making.

Here, we focus on real-world trading in stocks, commodities, currencies, futures, and options — using the art of Price Action Trading combined with the powerful tool of Volume Profile.

Stay connected with us to gain essential insights through:

  • Free articles, posts, and resources
  • Our paid course Trading with Price Action Volume
  • Self-learning access for free via VIP Access
